# AI Infrastructure PM Handbook

> Connecting AI and infrastructure fundamentals with product and business decisions.

A personal, evolving knowledge base for what I learn as an AI infrastructure PM. I often encounter a problem, study it, help solve it, and move on. This repository preserves the knowledge and reasoning before they fade.

The goal is to understand how technical constraints, customer needs, and economics shape product decisions—and reuse those lessons when the next problem arrives.

## What belongs here

| Area | Example topics |
|---|---|
| AI fundamentals | Models, training, inference, evaluation, and agents. |
| Infrastructure | GPUs, networking, storage, serving, KV cache, batching, routing, and observability. |
| Product management | Customer discovery, strategy, prioritization, metrics, adoption, and go-to-market. |
| AI infrastructure decisions | Performance and reliability targets, build versus buy, GPU costs, pricing, and unit economics. |
| Cases and lessons | Real problems, failed attempts, decisions, outcomes, and reusable insights. |

The handbook is a work in progress. Coverage will grow as new problems and lessons emerge.

## Modules

- [AI gateway](ai-gateway/README.md): connecting applications to model services, with a focus on product boundaries and operational tradeoffs.
- [MCP for complex systems](mcp/README.md): designing agent capabilities around business workflows, permissions, and useful user outcomes.
- [Evaluation](evaluation/README.md): evaluating capability contracts and intelligent behavior, from development through production.
